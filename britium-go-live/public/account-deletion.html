Request account and data deletion

For users of Britium Express Rider and Britium Express Enterprise.

You can request deletion without signing in or reinstalling either app.

How to send your request

  1. Email md@britiumventures.com with the subject Britium Express account and data deletion request.
  2. Tell us which app you use and the email address or phone number registered to your account. Say whether you want your account and associated data deleted, or only specific data removed.
  3. Send from your registered email address where possible. The team may need to verify account ownership before processing the request. If you cannot access that address, explain this in your message.
Email a deletion request

If the button does not open an email app, copy the address and subject into your email service and send the request there.

Do not send your password, one-time codes, identity documents, customer parcel lists or payment details. Opening this page or the email button does not delete anything. Your request is sent only when you send the email.

What the request covers

A full account deletion request covers your login account, profile and associated personal data, including contact information and any linked personal photos, location or activity records where applicable. You can also ask for specific data to be deleted while keeping your account.

Records that may need to be retained

Some delivery evidence, financial settlement records, audit records or security records may need to be retained for applicable legal obligations, fraud prevention or dispute resolution. A request does not automatically remove records that also belong to other customers or workforce members. The team must review these records and explain any applicable retention reason and period when handling your request.

Deletion requests are reviewed by the team; this page does not promise an automatic deletion or a fixed completion date. Ask about the status or applicable retention period by replying to the same email conversation.
