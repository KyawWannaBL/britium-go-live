Privacy Policy

Applies to: Britium Express Rider and Britium Express Enterprise
Effective date: 19 October 2026

Britium Ventures Company Limited operates the Britium Express logistics platform. This policy describes information processed when authorized users manage parcels, pickups, deliveries, customer service and related operational workflows through our applications.

Information processed

Depending on the service and role, the applications may process employee names, contact details, account identifiers and roles; parcel sender and recipient names, phone numbers and addresses; pickup and delivery coordinates; parcel photographs and delivery evidence; assignments, parcel statuses and operational remarks; COD amounts and settlement records; and technical information used to operate and protect the applications.

Purposes

We process this information to register and transport parcels, coordinate staff and routes, verify pickup and delivery, provide customer service, reconcile payments and resolve disputes, keep operational records, and maintain the security and reliability of our services.

Device permissions

Camera, photo access, location and notification permissions may be requested for features such as parcel verification, proof of delivery, navigation and operational alerts. Permission availability and controls depend on device settings and the feature used.

Sharing

Personal information may be accessed by authorized Britium Express personnel and service providers supporting necessary logistics functions, including hosting, authentication, storage, mapping and notifications. Access should be limited to the operational purpose.

Security

We use account authentication, access controls and other reasonable safeguards to protect operational information. No electronic service can promise absolute security.

Retention and requests

Information is retained as required for logistics services, financial reconciliation, customer inquiries, disputes, security and applicable obligations. To ask about access, correction or deletion, contact us below. Some records may need to be retained for legal and operational reasons.

Intended audience

These applications are designed for authorized business personnel and are not directed to children.

International processing

Service infrastructure or providers may process information outside Myanmar as necessary for application operations.

Changes

We may update this notice when our services or processing practices change, with the effective date updated on this page.
