# Daily branch financial monitoring

Yangon Head Office Finance monitors every active branch from the Finance portal's **Head Office daily branch Finance monitoring** panel. This includes Mandalay and future branches as they become active. Choose the business date in Myanmar time, refresh the report, and open a branch to inspect submissions and evidence.

Each branch submits its counted closing cash, expenses and funding/setup requirements through Branch Office → Administration & Finance. Set the business date explicitly, including when submitting a late report. A future counted cash close is rejected. Use a new reference for a corrected close and ask HQ Finance to reject the superseded report. Two unrejected closing reports are flagged for review and are never added together.

HQ Finance checks missing/rejected/multiple cash reports, expenses awaiting review, reviewed expense documents, outstanding positive-amount funding requests and pending document reviews. A pre-opening branch is identified separately until its configured opening date. Review the evidence and record the decision and reason. Authors cannot approve their own submissions.

Counted cash is not a verified bank deposit or COD receipt. An approved expense document is not proof of payment. Continue cash handover, COD reconciliation, merchant settlement and accounting posting through their existing Finance controls. Missing reports display as unknown rather than a zero balance. Warehouse intake, transfer receipt, wayplan review and mandatory dispatch scans retain their existing gates.

Network monitoring requires a Yangon Finance/accounting role or enterprise Superadmin. Other branch users retain their existing branch scope. The dashboard refreshes every 30 seconds while visible; it does not create payments, automatic approvals or scheduled notifications.
